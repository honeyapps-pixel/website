2. Description of the Service

Route2Bee is a mileage logbook app for mobile devices (iOS and Android) providing the following core functions:

  • Automatic trip detection via background GPS tracking
  • Manual trip entry
  • Categorization and management of trips
  • Mileage reports with PDF and CSV export
  • Cloud synchronization across your devices
  • Recordkeeping features including an audit log, trip finalization and export checksums

We may extend, modify or restrict the functionality of the App at any time. For a paid subscription period already begun, the core functions (automatic trip detection, export, cloud synchronization) remain available.

10. Mileage Records and Tax Matters

Route2Bee provides features intended to support contemporaneous mileage recordkeeping, including:

  • Audit log: every creation, edit, correction, finalization and deletion of a trip is recorded with a timestamp and the prior and new values.
  • Finalization: reviewed trips are marked as finalized; later edits are recorded as corrections, and finalized trips are not permanently deleted individually but flagged as deleted.
  • Timeliness prompts: the App flags manual entries made more than 7 days after the trip date.
  • Export checksum: PDF exports include a SHA-256 checksum supporting data integrity.

Recordkeeping is your responsibility. Any obligation to retain your mileage records (for example under IRS rules or your state’s rules) rests with you.

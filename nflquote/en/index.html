Football picks
with play money

Put points on every game of the season — moneyline, spread, over/under, team totals, winning margin, points ranges, overtime, player yards and touchdowns. 20 markets, parlays, and our own odds model anchored to the market. No purchase, no exchange, no payout.

Play money only: points have no cash value. There is no purchase, no exchange and no payout — details in the terms of use.

What’s inside

The markets every US book offers — priced from our own score distribution around spread and total, not copied from a sportsbook.

20 markets per game

Moneyline, spread with alternate lines to ±14, over/under with alternate totals, team totals, winner & total, winning margin bands, total points ranges, odd/even, overtime and winner after four quarters — and per player passing, rushing and receiving yards, receptions, touchdowns and kicker points, with lines from his own stats.

Straight bet or parlay

Several games on one slip — or several markets on a single game, honestly priced on the joint probability instead of the product of the individual odds.

Odds from our own model

A score distribution per game, centered by the market and aware of football’s key numbers — 3 and 7 are not numbers like any other. American or decimal, whichever you read.

Leagues with friends

Start your own league or join with a link. Every league keeps its own balance and its own standings — plus a worldwide ranking.

Standings with playoff seeds

The divisions by win percentage, the seven playoff spots per conference, and for every game the win probability including overtime.

Cash out early

An open slip can be cashed out before the end — at the current value of your games still open.

Daily check-in

One point a day per league account, three from a seven-day streak on. Plus a weekly bonus every Monday — no strings attached.

A ledger, not a black box

Every transaction can be looked up. If a result is corrected afterwards, the app resettles — and tells you.

What it looks like

Six looks into the app — the week, player props, a game, the slip, the standings and your league.
